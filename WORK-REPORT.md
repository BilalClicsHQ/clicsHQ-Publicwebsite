## 💳 Payments & Billing — Polar (Selected Platform)

The client has finalized **Polar (polar.sh)** for pricing & payments. Here is a simple guide:

### What Polar Covers
- **Card payments** — Visa, Mastercard, etc. work worldwide (customers can pay from any country)
- **Subscriptions** — monthly / yearly plans (matches our Pricing page exactly)
- **One-time payments** and usage-based billing also supported
- **Taxes handled for us** — Polar is a "Merchant of Record", so they manage all international sales tax / VAT automatically. We don't deal with tax paperwork in any country.
- **Customer portal** — customers can manage/cancel their own subscriptions
- Trusted by known companies (Tailwind CSS, Midday)

### Will We Get Paid? ✅ Yes
- Polar officially supports payouts to **Pakistan** and **UAE** (bank payouts via Stripe)
- Note: account approval takes **~2 weeks** before first payout — should start the application early

### Fees (Simple Breakdown)
| Plan | Cost | Fee per sale |
|------|------|--------------|
| Starter | Free | 5% + $0.50 |
| Pro | $20/month | 3.8% + $0.40 |
- +1.5% extra on non-US cards; ~$2/month + 0.25% payout fee
- Example: on a $12 sale → roughly $11.20–11.40 reaches us

### How Hard Is Setup? — Easy
- Official **Next.js integration** (our website's exact technology) — plug-and-play
- Checkout can even work with simple payment links (no code needed to start)
- Test "sandbox" mode available — we can test everything before going live
- Estimated work: **1–2 days** to connect our Pricing page fully

### Possible Issues (Honest View)
- ⏳ ~2 week account verification wait (start early)
- 💰 Fees are higher than Stripe directly — but Stripe is **not available in Pakistan**, so a Merchant of Record like Polar is the practical option
- 📄 Digital products/SaaS only (fine for us)
- ⚠ Customer disputes cost $15 each, win or lose

**Verdict:** Good fit for clicsHQ — handles cards, subscriptions, and taxes globally, pays out to Pakistan/UAE, and integrates easily with our website.
